Hackathon 📍 Remote - Austria

BR41NIO Hackathon: Building NeuroSecure 🧠🔐

Over 700 attendees, and only one of them is Bosnian — me! ✨

Built "NeuroSecure" — a biometric authentication system powered by EEG data and ML classification models for cybersecurity enhancement in medical facilities.

Half the team did ML in Python, I handled the scary backend in raw MATLAB code (~300 lines for the mock-up). 😬

Looking forward to many more engineering adventures from Amina's RGB lab. Until then — stay curious. 🌊

Life Milestone 📍 A New Chapter

"Is Getting a PhD Worth It?" — YES, With Purpose 💖

Discovered my AuDHD diagnosis right before PhD enrollment. My brain runs on two operating systems: autism craves order, ADHD thrives in chaos.

Decided to dedicate this stage of my life to those with no system or community to support them.

"We are not broken, but different by design, and just as valuable as anyone else."

I chose to fight prejudice and stigma through academic development. Find your purpose and fight for it.
