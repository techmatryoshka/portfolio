Get to knowAmina ✦

I'm a PhD student in Biomedical Engineering with a love for building neuroadaptive systems that help neurodivergent brains thrive. I approach research with curiosity, hands-on experimentation, and a collaborative mindset.

I started my research journey as a bioengineer, mainly focused on molecular biology and hardcore lab work. Until I grew super tired of it and got rid of this "bio" in "bioengineering", and decided to try my luck in engineering — extreme transformation and alteration in my career, yes, but an incredibly rewarding one!

Beyond the technical aspects, my own neurodivergent perspective shapes the questions I ask, the problems I notice, and the solutions I pursue. I love connecting dots across disciplines: AI, neuroscience, psychology, ethics, and building systems that are as human-aware as they are technically robust.

"Being neurodivergent myself, the idea of having no community or ways to get support, just because my brain operates a bit differently — I have endeavored myself to the greatest adventure and quest of my life: create the community through academic research."

✦ My Neurodivergent Journey ✦

Odd By Design

I have AuDHD — which means my brain runs on two operating systems. The discovery of that state came right before my PhD enrollment.

Autism

Craves order, patterns, deep focus, and systematic thinking

⚡

ADHD

Thrives in chaos, novelty, hyperfocus bursts, and creative leaps

My PhD research has evolved toward designing non-invasive neuroadaptive systems for neurodivergent individuals, focusing especially on ADHD and autism. I'm fascinated by how biosignals can inform interventions that improve attention, emotional regulation, and overall cognitive wellbeing.

"Mind that desires order but rejoices in chaos never settles for mediocrity." 💖
