Focus Areas

AI in Medical Diagnostics

Developing deep learning models—particularly 3D LSTM networks—for multimodal biosignal analysis, stress detection, and diagnostic decision support systems.

Exploring EEG-based systems from biometric authentication (NeuroSecure) to therapeutic BCI applications, making neural signals actionable for real-world use.

Securing healthcare systems through innovative approaches—from ethical hacking methodologies to brainwave-based biometric authentication for sensitive patient data.
